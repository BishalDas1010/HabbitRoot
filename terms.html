HabitRoot is a habit-building and focus app made by Bishal Das, an independent developer. It’s designed for anyone who wants to study better, stay focused, and build consistent daily routines. You’re always in control:

App blocking is a helper, not a guarantee. It may not work perfectly on every phone, because Android updates, battery-saving modes, and manufacturer settings can interfere. Please don’t rely on HabitRoot as your only way to stay away from distracting apps or to handle important tasks.

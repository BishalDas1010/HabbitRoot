FAQ

Got Questions?

Here are some common questions about HabitRoot.

Is HabitRoot free to use?

Yes, HabitRoot is completely free. There are no in-app purchases or ads.

When will HabitRoot be available on Google Play?

We're working on it. Stay tuned for updates!

Does HabitRoot block all apps?

No, only the apps you select for your focus sessions.

Can I use HabitRoot offline?

Yes, the app works fully offline.
